Astro Recommender

Ranks deep sky objects for tonight at your location. Enter your latitude and longitude, or use your location, and press Run; an empty field keeps its default. Point at or tap a result for its picture and altitude through the night.

Runs entirely in your browser. The only network requests go to the forecast, sky brightness, geocoding and comet services, to NOAA for the solar flux (no location sent), to CDS for the DSS2 thumbnail when you point at a result, and to Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless visit counter that sees the page address (your form, after a run).
